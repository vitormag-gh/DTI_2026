Dipole Moment (μ): This is a measure of the separation of positive and negative electrical charges within a molecule. It's a vector quantity, indicating both the magnitude and the direction of the molecule's polarity.

Polarizability (α): This refers to the ability of a molecule to become polarized in the presence of an external electric field. It is a measure of how easily the electron cloud around a molecule can be distorted.

HOMO-LUMO Gap: This is the energy difference between the highest occupied molecular orbital (HOMO) and the lowest unoccupied molecular orbital (LUMO). It's an important indicator of a molecule's chemical stability and its electronic properties.
